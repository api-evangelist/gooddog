# Good Dog

> Good Dog is a marketplace that connects people looking for a dog with a vetted national network of ethical breeders, animal shelters, and rescues. The platform screens sources against veterinarian-developed standards, processes payments securely on-platform, and supports owners with a Learning Center, Care Specialists, and pet insurance options. Over 250,000 families have found a dog through Good Dog. No public API or developer program is published as of this writing.
